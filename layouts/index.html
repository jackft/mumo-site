[mumo]

A web-based tool for multimodal interaction analysis. Transcribe, time-align, and annotate conversation.

Download — Try the webapp

What it does

Transcription
Multimodal transcription for linguistics, conversation analysis, animal behavior, and other fields.
Annotation tiers
Define tiers for speech, gesture, gaze, and other modalities. Compatible with ELAN's tier and constraint model.
Conversation analysis support
Built for CA work: overlapping speech, repair sequences, adjacency pairs, and turn construction units.
Timeline
Synchronized waveform and annotation timeline. Snapping to audio features and other annotations.
Export
Export to SQLite for analysis, or to EAF for compatibility with existing workflows.

Project status

mumo is under active development. The core transcript editor and timeline are functional. Annotation frames and export are in progress.
